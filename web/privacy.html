Privacy Policy — Air Hockey

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Air Hockey 2P ("we", "our", or "the application", package dev.vmellos.airhockey) is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled across Android devices.

1. Fundamental Principles

  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identity information, individual user profiles, or usage telemetry.
  • 100% Offline & Peer-to-Peer Architecture: Gameplay runs entirely without relying on external cloud servers or remote database infrastructure. Multiplayer Extended Table matches establish direct socket connections over local Wi-Fi or Mobile Hotspot.
  • Strictly Local Storage: Match history records, competitive Elo leaderboard scores, and local preferences remain solely stored within your device's isolated local sandbox.

2. Requested Permissions & Technical Purposes

Permission Technical Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Used exclusively for peer-to-peer Sockets (TCP/UDP) communication between two devices on the same local Wi-Fi or Hotspot network.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Monitors wireless network state to discover the physical game table.
android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Resolves the host device IP address in Hotspot mode without requiring manual user input.
android.permission.CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE Enables mDNS/NSD protocol for zero-configuration local network discovery.
android.permission.NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES Discovers nearby devices over local Wi-Fi with neverForLocation flag on modern Android versions (API 31+).
android.permission.VIBRATE Delivers haptic vibration feedback upon hitting the puck and scoring goals.
android.permission.CAMERA Used strictly on demand to scan the game table QR Code. No camera images or video feeds are ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.

3. Advertising & Regulatory Compliance (Google AdMob & UMP)

To support continuous development and offer the game completely free of charge, the application may display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob (bottom banners in menu screens and frequency-capped interstitials upon match completion). The application integrates Google's official User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK, in full compliance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Brazilian LGPD, and the ePrivacy Directive.

4. Children's Privacy

Air Hockey is rated for all ages (Everyone / Family). The application does not contain harmful content, does not target minors maliciously, and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
