Privacy Policy — Air Hockey

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Air Hockey 2P ("we", "our", or "the application", package dev.vmellos.airhockey) is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled across Android devices.

1. Fundamental Principles

  • Local-First Gameplay & Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer: Matches run locally without proprietary game servers. Extended Table and Connected Table matches establish direct local TCP/UDP socket connections over Wi-Fi or Mobile Hotspot.
  • Strictly Local Match Storage: Match history records, stage progression, and local preferences remain stored within your device's isolated sandbox (Room SQLite & Jetpack DataStore).
  • No Mandatory Login: All offline and local multiplayer modes work without creating an account.

2. Requested Permissions & Technical Purposes

Permission Technical Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Used for local TCP/UDP peer-to-peer multiplayer sockets as well as Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Google Play Games Services.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Monitors wireless network state to discover local game tables and manage online SDK connectivity.
android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Resolves the host device IP address in Hotspot mode without requiring manual user input.
android.permission.CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE Enables mDNS/NSD protocol for zero-configuration local network discovery.
android.permission.NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES Discovers nearby devices over local Wi-Fi with neverForLocation flag on Android 13+.
android.permission.ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK Authorizes local network communication for Wi-Fi/Hotspot table discovery on modern Android versions.
android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Scoped strictly to Android 12 and below (maxSdkVersion="32") as required by legacy Android APIs for local Wi-Fi/mDNS discovery; never used for GPS tracking.
android.permission.VIBRATE Delivers haptic vibration feedback upon hitting the puck and scoring goals.
android.permission.CAMERA Used strictly on demand to scan the game table QR Code. No camera images or video feeds are ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.
com.android.vending.BILLING Processes the optional one-time in-app purchase (remove_ads) securely via Google Play Billing.

3. Advertising, In-App Purchases & Play Games Services

To offer the game free of charge, the application displays non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob (bottom banners on menu screens and frequency-capped post-match interstitials, never during active gameplay). Google's official User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK manages regional consent in compliance with GDPR, LGPD, and the ePrivacy Directive. AdMob may process device identifiers (Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction diagnostics subject to your consent settings.

An optional non-consumable one-time purchase (remove_ads) is available via Google Play Billing to permanently disable ads; we never access or store payment card data. If you optionally sign in with Google Play Games Services v2, stage progression, stats, and preferences may be backed up via Play Games Cloud Saves (SnapshotsClient) and Elo/stage scores submitted to global leaderboards under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Air Hockey is rated for all ages (Everyone / Family). The application does not contain harmful content and does not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13.

Última atualização: 17 de novembro de 2026

O Air Hockey 2P ("nós", "nosso" ou "o aplicativo", pacote dev.vmellos.airhockey) tem como compromisso a transparência e a proteção da privacidade dos seus usuários.

1. Princípios Fundamentais

  • Jogabilidade Local-First e Multijogador P2P: As partidas rodam localmente sem servidores proprietários. Os modos Mesa Estendida e Mesa Conectada estabelecem conexão direta via sockets TCP/UDP em Wi-Fi local ou Ponto de Acesso Móvel (Hotspot).

  • Sem Cadastro Obrigatório: Todos os modos offline e multijogador local funcionam sem criar conta ou login.

2. Permissões Solicitadas e Justificativas Técnicas

3. Publicidade, Compras no App e Google Play Games

Para manter o jogo gratuito, o aplicativo exibe anúncios não invasivos fornecidos pelo Google AdMob (banners nos menus e intersticiais com limitação de frequência no encerramento de partidas, nunca durante o jogo ativo). A plataforma oficial de consentimento do Google (User Messaging Platform - UMP) gerencia permissões em conformidade com a LGPD, o GDPR e a Diretiva ePrivacy. O Air Hockey é classificado para todas as idades (Classificação Livre / Everyone). O aplicativo não direciona conteúdo prejudicial nem coleta conscientemente dados pessoais diretamente de crianças menores de 13 anos.
